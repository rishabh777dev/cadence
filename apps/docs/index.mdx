Cadence is the voice dictation app that turns your voice into text with the press of a hotkey. Speak 4X faster than you type. 

It's open source and local first. You can choose what models to use, download local models or external ones with BYOK. We also provide cloud transcription options. 

## What you can do

- **Voice dictation:** hold the hotkey, speak, release to paste at wherever your cursor is. Dictate directly into your favorite coding agent. 
- **Choose transcription:** Choose between local voice models or BYOK.
- **Transcription cleaning:** Grammar and punctuation cleanup via post-processing. No more "um, oh, but".
- **Dictionary:** Custom phrase replacements applied after transcription (e.g. "type script" → TypeScript).
- **Cadence Plugins SDK:** Build custom workflows in the transcription pipeline with our SDK.
